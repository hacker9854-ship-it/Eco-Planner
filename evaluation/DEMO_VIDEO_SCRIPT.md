## 🎬 Audio & Video Recording Checklist

1. **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD).
2. **Audio**: Use a clear microphone with zero background noise.
3. **Cursor**: Enable mouse click effects in Loom/OBS so judges see exactly which buttons you are clicking.
4. **Browser**: Press `F11` for clean full-screen browser mode without distracting desktop icons or taskbar.
5. **Pacing**: Speak at a steady, confident, energetic pace without rushing.
